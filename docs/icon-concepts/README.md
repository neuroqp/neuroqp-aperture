# NeuroQP Aperture icon concepts

Five concepts generated using the built-in imagegen tool. These are design candidates for selection.

Brand reference: `neuroqp/apps/web/public/brand/neuroqp-logo-icon-transparent.png` in the main NeuroQP repository. Palette sampled directly from that icon: purple `#6C3E89` and orange `#F7830D`. The source logo itself contains gradients; these concepts target broad shapes and minimal detail.

All five PNGs preserve generated transparency. Reviewed at 16, 32, and 64 pixels on light and dark backgrounds. These are raster concepts; exact palette normalization and final platform icon exports can follow selection.

## Shared prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: NeuroQP Aperture desktop application icon and favicon, square canvas.
Primary request: Generate exactly ONE ultra-minimal icon concept. It must remain clear at 16px and 32px, with a bold silhouette, broad filled shapes and generous negative space.
Style/medium: perfectly clean flat vector-like raster graphic with crisp smooth edges, very low detail.
Color palette: ONLY NeuroQP logo purple #6C3E89 and NeuroQP logo orange #F7830D. These are sampled from the real NeuroQP brand icon. Both colors must be visible and clearly separated. No other colored fills.
Composition/framing: centered single standalone symbol, roughly 78 percent of canvas width and height, balanced padding on all four sides, square 1024 by 1024 output.
Scene/backdrop: actual transparent background including the central cutouts.
Constraints: no words, letters, labels, captions, watermark, mockup, sheet, extra icons, outer app tile, fine strokes, shading, gradient, glow, shadows, bevel, texture or 3D. Use only a few large shapes.
```

## Concept prompts

### Six-blade aperture

```text
Subject: An actual camera lens aperture, six broad interlocking shutter blades arranged in a circular pinwheel around a large transparent central hexagonal opening. Alternating purple and orange blade fills, exactly three of each. Flat continuous circular outer silhouette. Thick simple blades, central opening about one third of total diameter. A physically recognizable camera diaphragm rather than a flower. No thin internal lines.
```

### Four-blade iris

```text
Subject: An actual camera lens aperture with exactly four broad shutter blades forming a compact rounded-square outer silhouette and a large transparent central diamond-shaped opening. Top and bottom blades purple, left and right blades orange. Each blade is one simple filled geometric shape, clean interlocking diagonal seams. Strong fourfold symmetry, no extra outer ring. Recognizable square iris diaphragm, angular and architectural, visibly different from a round six-blade shutter.
```

### Focused cell

```text
Subject: A single large orange disk representing a microscopy cell, held within four very thick purple L-shaped focus brackets. Only five filled elements total. Disk centered, brackets form the four corners of an implied square with wide breaks at the midpoint of every side. Corner tips gently rounded. No crosshair, no nucleus, no tiny dots, no rays, no aperture blades. An immediately readable minimal image-viewing and microscopy symbol.
```

### Q lens

```text
Subject: A bold Q-shaped optical lens emblem. One very thick purple circular ring surrounding a large transparent circular opening, and one short broad orange diagonal tail at its lower right, angled 45 degrees outward. Tail cleanly overlaps the ring to suggest the Q from NeuroQP and a compact magnifying lens, without becoming a long-handled magnifying glass. Only two shapes. Smooth round terminations. No iris blades, extra dots or details.
```

### Slice stack

```text
Subject: Two overlapping microscopy image slices, shown as two broad flat rounded diamond-shaped plates aligned diagonally. Back plate purple, shifted upper-left; front plate orange, shifted lower-right. Front orange diamond has one large transparent diamond cutout so it becomes a very thick frame, revealing a portion of the purple slice behind. Two shapes and one cutout only. Strong compact stepped silhouette, gently rounded corners, no perspective shading or fine layers, no cell details, no aperture blades.
```
