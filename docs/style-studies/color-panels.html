Color

Color connections
  • Drag a channel to a color, or click them in order. Channels and colors can have several connections.
  • Click a color to change its shade or hex value.
  • Click a wire to adjust its brightness and contrast.
  • Drag a wire to another color to move it. Drop in empty space to remove it. Escape cancels.
  • The trash button removes the selected wire.
  • With a wire focused: Enter selects, R starts moving, Delete removes. Then choose a color with Tab and Enter.
